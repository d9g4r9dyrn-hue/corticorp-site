How it works

A staff with a chain of command, not one model with a long prompt

The things that make a human organization dependable also work for AI agents: narrow jobs, limited access, written hand-offs, review by someone else, and a record of who said what. This is the organization that writes, checks and improves Corticorp News and Corticorp Finance every day.

ADelegation

Each role has one job and sees only what that job needs. One role writes code. One role reaches the web. Nobody does everything.

BCoordination

Work moves in writing. A finding becomes a work item, a work item becomes a pull request, and the team has filed more than 760 items on itself.

COversight

Propose, never ship. The merge, the deploy and the production keys are out of every AI role’s reach by construction, not by request.

DAccountability

Every fund’s reasoning, every Board finding and every item the operator declined is published. The team is scored on what it said.

opd the Open Page Document

An open file format meant to replace PDF in the common case: one .opd file carries a clean, reflowable source in a strict subset of HTML, its fonts, and optionally a frozen copy of the exact pages, bound to the source by hash. Around it, a viewer, an editor and a PDF importer that are each a single HTML file, run offline from any folder, and never phone home. Write a document, add form fields, send it, have it filled and signed, and collect the returned copies. It opens Word and PDF files and downloads as Word, OpenDocument or PDF.

World’s Eye View

Around 53,000 public webcams from traffic authorities, observatories and weather networks on five continents, quilted onto one satellite map as live thumbnails: volcanoes, harbours, city streets, ocean buoys and an Antarctic research station. Background

About

I’m Cort — a software engineer with 25+ years building systems for Fortune 500 companies and startups across finance, energy, healthcare, and marketing.

For most of that time the work was conventional engineering: design the system, build it, test it, ship it, keep it running. When AI models got good enough to do real work, I started by putting one at the center of an application instead of bolting it on: a model scoring rental photos in Apartment Finder, another writing one summary per real-world event in Corticorp News. Then the projects outgrew a single model. Finance became 44 simulated fund managers, each with its own discipline and its own published reasons, and keeping two sites honest every day became the job of a staff: a Board that reads the code, a Newsroom that reads the data, and me deciding what ships.

That is what CortiCorp builds now. When this site says “we”, it means me and the organizations I direct, not a large human staff. And none of it works without the old disciplines: data models, tests, audits, deploy paths and someone accountable for every change. The agents do the work. The engineering is what makes the work worth trusting.

AI organizations

Systems where Claude models hold defined roles — writing, scoring, auditing and reviewing each other’s work — with a human decision point and a public record of what each one said.

Data Engineering

Scraping, cleaning, enriching, and visualizing data from messy real-world sources. Turning unstructured listings, photos, and text into searchable, scored, actionable dashboards.

Audio & DSP

Real-time audio synthesis in C++ with JUCE. Oscillator design, filter modeling, physical-string simulation, zero-allocation audio threads, and shipping a full VST3 plugin.

Full Stack

End-to-end: Python backends, interactive web dashboards, REST APIs, cloud deployment, and desktop applications. Whatever the project needs to ship.
