The Spies Cookbook

Granny's recipes, declassified.

A family cookbook filed like a case folder. Each spread pairs one of Granny's recipes with a story or a record from the family's wartime service.

It draws on the same research as the WW2 book in Shows, Places & Big Ideas, and nobody else has the source material.

Kanazawa Ice
Biotherapy Development Research Center, since 2017. Japanese ice cream and popsicles that hold their shape in the heat, thanks to a strawberry extract. The link shows the extract.

40V max XGT Portable Microwave
Makita, since 2023. A cordless microwave that runs on power-tool batteries. Portable rather than handheld.

Ovens with a temperature probe
GE Appliances. Ovens with a plug-in meat probe that shut off when the food reaches the set temperature. Other makers offer the same.
products.geappliances.com
