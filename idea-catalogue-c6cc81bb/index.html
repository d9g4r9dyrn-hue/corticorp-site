From the editor

Read this as fiction. It is a catalogue of ideas, not a plan: hardware, software, businesses, shows and names, written down as they arrived and drawn here so they can be looked at. Most of it is fantasy, the kind of thing that belongs in a story or a sci-fi catalogue. Nothing is for sale and nothing here claims to work.

Some of these things exist now. If somebody already made one, good: that means it can be bought. Some never could work. A few still might, and one, the car in Workshop & Garage, is on its way to being real. Nothing in these pages is judged; that is a later pass.

New ideas get a place when they arrive.
